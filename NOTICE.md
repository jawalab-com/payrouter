# NOTICE — Brand Marks Used in the Hosted Checkout UI

This project distributes the official logos of several banks, e-wallets, retail
outlets, and card schemes inside `internal/server/checkoutui/assets/logos/` so
the hosted checkout page can display them inline (self-contained, no CDN, no
extra HTTP requests).

## Trademark notice

All marks are the property of their respective owners. They are included and
displayed **solely to indicate payment-method compatibility** ("this checkout
accepts BCA Virtual Account"), which is referential use — they do not imply
endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation with PayRouter or the merchant. Brand
owners may request removal of their mark at any time.
